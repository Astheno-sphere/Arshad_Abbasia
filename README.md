## Proof, not claims — three real films

- **Molde, Monday morning:** 289 trips, 5,963 km, peak 67 at 07:35. 1 in 5 trips is a ferry, the sea carries 8% of the distance.
- **Oslofjord, in a gale:** deck air +24 °C where the crew breathe. 2 vessels, 6 sites, 113 km in 5.6 h.
- **The ferry multiplier:** timetabled Γ=2/3: up to 30% (certified upper bound). Additive: 0–0.38%.
